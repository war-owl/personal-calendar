Karan Personal Calendar

A private, single-user tool

Karan Personal Calendar is a small script that its owner, Karan Bhardwj, runs on his own computer to view and manage events in his personal Google Calendar.

It is not a public service. There are no sign-ups, and it only connects to the owner's own Google account. It reads his calendar list and events, and creates, edits or deletes events when he asks it to.

See the privacy policy for exactly what it accesses and where that data goes.
